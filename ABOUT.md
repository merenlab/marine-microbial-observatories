# About

## What this is

This is a living catalogue of the programs that generate molecular data on marine microbes,
such as long-running stations, large-scale expeditions, time-series observatories, and the
networks that aim to coordinate observing efforts.

This resource is a by-product of the [Marine Microbial Observatories for the Future](https://merenlab.org/workshops/MMOFF/)
workshop that took place at the [Helmholtz Institute for Functional Marine Biodiversity](https://hifmb.de) (HIFMB) in Oldenburg,
Germany.

The catalogue is meant to track programs which start, stop, change contacts, and move
their data between archives. Every record carries a 'verification' block naming who last
confirmed the accuracy of the entry and when, so it is always possible to tell how stale a given entry is.

We welcome new entries or updates to existing ones! Please see our [contribution](#contribute) to see how
you can get involved.

## Downloads

The following tables are Generated from the current records on every change:

- [time-series.tsv](https://microbial-observatories.org/time-series.tsv)
- [expeditions.tsv](https://microbial-observatories.org/expeditions.tsv)
- [coordination-networks.tsv](https://microbial-observatories.org/coordination-networks.tsv)
- [data.json](https://microbial-observatories.org/data.json) (everything, including fields not present in the TSV exports).

The TSV exports flatten lists into `; `-separated strings for spreadsheet users. They are a
convenience, not the source of truth; `data.json` is the faithful representation, and it
carries the licence and build provenance in its top-level `licence` key.


## Contact and corrections

Anything wrong, out of date, or missing? [Tell us](CONTRIBUTING.md) by email, through an
issue form, or as a pull request. All three are equally welcome, and corrections are as
valuable as new records.

If you are listed as a contact and would rather not be, email meren@hifmb.de and you will
be removed.
